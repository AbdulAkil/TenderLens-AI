# TenderLens AI
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Belgian SME owners and bid teams discovering suitable public procurement contracts.
Initial validation assumption: IT/software and cybersecurity businesses, as permitted
by the supplied brief. Profiles support other industries.

## Product Purpose
Deliver relevant tender opportunities automatically, with source evidence and clear
uncertainty, so customers can decide where to spend bid preparation time.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve Python/FastAPI/PostgreSQL and Next.js. Useful without paid AI. No invented
tenders, customer claims or legal compliance claims. Live services require founder
activation. Pricing hypotheses: Starter €29, Professional €79, Business €149 monthly.

## Brand Commitments
Professional European B2B service; restrained color, legibility, accessible forms,
opportunities and next actions over charts. Decisions delegated by the user's brief.

## Evidence on Hand
Official TED ingestion and one reviewed retrieval example; no customer testimonials
or validated matching performance claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA principles; keyboard navigation, responsive web, English/Dutch translation
architecture and French expansion. Legal copy requires professional review.
